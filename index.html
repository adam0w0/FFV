First time playing FFV? Start here.

The Job system is the whole game. You can freely switch each character's class at almost any time from the menu. Experiment often; there is no wrong choice and swapping is free.

Blue Magic has rules worth knowing up front. Only one character needs to learn a spell for everyone to be able to use it later, and you must win the battle to keep it, since fleeing loses it. Many spells will not simply be cast at you: some need a Beastmaster's Control to force the enemy to use them, and others only fire under a specific condition. The hints say which.

Keep a Thief in the party early. Thieves reveal hidden passages and let you dash. Several secrets cannot be seen without one.

Watch for the red Heads-up hints. FF5 has a few moments that lock content away for good, above all the two crossings between worlds. Those hints tell you what to finish before it is too late.

How the hints work: each entry starts hidden behind a safe title. Tap to reveal one line at a time, vague first, exact answer last. Secrets like jobs and summons are only named on that final tap.
